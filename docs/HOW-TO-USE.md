# 🍅 Your cozy guide to Pomodoro Cute

One task, one tomato, one little win. You don't need to finish everything at once. 💛

Need help getting the app open? Start with [the installation guide](INSTALLATION.md).

Already set up? Double-click **Launch Pomodoro.cmd** to use the source app, or
**dist\portable\Pomodoro Cute.exe** to use the portable copy. After code changes,
save your files and use **Build Portable.cmd** to refresh that portable copy.

## 🌱 Your first session in five little steps

1. Click **📝 Tasks**. Type a small task, such as **Read one chapter**, then click **Add**.
2. On its card, choose **In Progress** from the status menu.
3. Click **Focus on this**. The timer now shows your task under **Working on**.
4. Click **Start** and give that one task your attention.
5. When the timer finishes, add a little note if you like, then click **Save & continue**.

Try a note like:

> Read pages 1–12 and wrote three key ideas. Next time: finish the chapter.

Finished the whole task? Tick **Mark this task as completed** before saving. It moves to **Completed**. ✨

Your session is now in **📖 Journal**, and its focus time appears on the task card.

## 🏡 A desktop that makes room for you

Drag a window edge to make the app wider, narrower, taller, or shorter. You can
also maximize it. Your preferred window size and last visited page are remembered
when you reopen the app.

Use **Focus**, **Tasks**, **Journal**, **Stats**, and **Settings** in the navigation
sidebar. In a narrower window, navigation moves to the top and the timer becomes
a compact strip. The timer stays available as you move between pages.

The **Focus** page shows today's sessions, focus time, completed tasks, and a few
upcoming tasks. **Focus →** selects one of them on the timer; **Start** begins it.

Each page scrolls within its workspace, keeping navigation and the timer nearby.
Task details and session notes open in small dialogs when you need them.

## ⏰ Meet your timer

The default rhythm is **25 minutes of focus**, followed by a **5-minute break**. After every **4 completed focus sessions**, you get a **15-minute long break**.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Start** | Starts the current focus or break phase. |
| **Pause** | Pauses the timer so you can come back to it. |
| **↺ Reset** | Restarts the current phase from its full duration. |
| **⏭ Skip** | Moves to the next work/break phase. |

When a focus session ends, the timer switches to a break. Save your session details, then click **Start** when you're ready to begin the break. Breaks also wait for you to start them.

> 🍃 **Pause for interruptions.** Resetting or skipping an unfinished focus session doesn't add it to the journal. Completed focus sessions are the ones that count.

### Choose what you're working on

Before starting, choose a task from **Working on**, or use **Focus on this** from its task card.

You can also leave **Unassigned focus** selected. A focus session is still worth saving, even without a task!

Once a session starts, its task selection stays locked, including while paused. You can adjust the task in the completion form when the session ends.

## 📝 Give your tasks a little home

Click **Tasks** to open your three-column board:

| Column | A simple way to think about it |
| --- | --- |
| **To Do** | Things waiting for your attention. |
| **In Progress** | Things you're working on. |
| **Completed** | Little wins you've finished. 🎉 |

### Add a task

Type its title in the field at the top and click **Add**. That's all you need.

Small tasks are easier to start: **Draft the introduction** feels friendlier than **Finish the entire project**.

### Move or reopen a task

Use the **status menu on its card** to choose another column. To reopen a completed task, choose **To Do** or **In Progress**.

You can also drag a card by its **⠿ grip** into another column. Use **Find a little
task…** above the board to search titles and descriptions.

### Edit a task or see its progress

Click the **task title** to open its details. Here you can:

- Change its title.
- Add an optional description.
- Change its status and click **Save task**.
- See its completed session count, total focus time, last worked date, and session notes.

### Delete a task

Open its details and click **Delete task**. Its journal entries remain available, including the task title saved with each session.

Renaming a task also keeps the original titles in older journal entries, so your work diary stays meaningful.

## ✨ Finish a session your way

When your focus timer finishes, the **A little progress!** form appears.

Your completed session is **already saved**. You can now:

1. Confirm the task you worked on, choose a different task, or leave it unassigned.
2. Add optional notes about what you accomplished.
3. Tick **Mark this task as completed**, if you're done with it.
4. Click **Save & continue**.

No notes today? Leave the box empty and continue. There are no extra required fields. 🌷

The **×** button also saves the form's current fields and closes it.

> 💾 Save the form before quitting if you want to keep the notes you've just typed. If you quit before finishing the form, the completed session remains saved and the form reopens next time; unsaved typed notes aren't restored.

## 📖 Browse your little work diary

Click **Journal** to see your saved focus sessions.

- **Day** shows one day's sessions.
- **Week** shows a Monday-to-Sunday week.
- **Month** shows a calendar month.
- The **date picker** chooses a date to explore.
- **‹** and **›** move to the previous or next day, week, or month.
- **Today** brings you back to the current date.

Each entry shows its task, start/end times, focus duration, notes, and any task completion event. The summary adds up sessions and focus minutes for the selected period.

Sessions appear on the **local date they finished**. A session that ends after midnight belongs to the new day.

### Tidy up your notes

Click **Edit notes** on a journal entry, update the text, and click **Save & continue**.

Reopening a task keeps its earlier completion event in the diary. Editing that old entry's notes won't complete the reopened task again.

## 📤 Take your progress with you

Inside **Journal**:

1. Choose the date you want to report on.
2. Expand **Export journal** near the bottom.
3. Choose **Selected day**, **Selected week**, **Selected month**, **Custom dates**, or **All history**.
4. For custom dates, set **From** and **Through**. Both dates are included.
5. Choose a format and click **Export**.
6. Choose where to save your file in the Windows save dialog.

| Format | Lovely for… |
| --- | --- |
| **Markdown (.md)** | A readable work report, organized by date and task. |
| **CSV (.csv)** | Opening your activity in Excel or another spreadsheet app. |
| **JSON (.json)** | Using session data with other tools. |

Exports include session dates/times, tasks, focus duration, notes, and task completion events. They export sessions; they aren't a complete backup of your task board and settings.

## 🎨 Make it feel like you

Open **⚙ Settings** to choose:

- Focus, short-break, and long-break lengths.
- How many focus sessions earn a long break.
- Sound and notification preferences.
- **Tomato, Mint, Blueberry, Peach, or Lavender** colors.
- **Light**, **Dark**, or **Follow system** appearance.

Settings save automatically when changed. An existing focus session keeps its original duration, even if you change the work length while it's running or paused.

### A tiny timer companion 🧸

Click **Tiny timer** in the navigation area to enter mini mode. It shows the
timer and Start/Pause in a small window that stays on top of other windows.

Click **⤢** in the mini window's top corner to expand it again. Your previous
desktop window size is restored. When a focus session ends in mini mode, the app
expands so you can save its details.

### A quick peek at your progress 📊

Open **Stats** for daily, weekly, or monthly charts and focus totals. Use **Journal** when you want to read the individual sessions and notes behind those numbers.

## ⌨️ Handy little shortcuts

Use these when you're not typing or working inside a task-details or session-notes dialog:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Space** | Start or pause the timer, when focus isn't on a button. |
| **R** | Reset the current phase. |
| **S** | Open or close statistics. |
| **Esc** | Close an open panel. In the completion form, this saves its current fields. |
| **Ctrl + 1–5** | Switch to Focus, Tasks, Journal, Stats, or Settings. |
| **Alt + ←** | Return to the previous page. |

**Space** and **R** shortcuts work on the Focus page. On any page, you can still
click the timer's controls directly. **Esc** from a workspace page returns to Focus.

## 🌙 Come back tomorrow

Closing the window hides the app in the system tray; a running timer keeps going. Click its tray icon near the Windows clock to show it again.

To fully exit, right-click the tray icon and choose **Quit**.

Your saved tasks, sessions, notes, and settings remain on this computer. Running and paused timers also recover when you reopen the app. If a running focus session finishes while the app is fully closed, it is recorded when you next open the app.

If you see a storage error, follow the message and export readable journal entries to keep a copy. The app preserves existing stored data instead of silently replacing it.
